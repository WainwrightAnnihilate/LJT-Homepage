In parameter-efficient finetuning (PEFT), a lightweight module is learned on a specific dataset while the underlying pretrained model remains unchanged. In this paper, we propose to compose these parameter-efficient modules through linear arithmetic operations in the weight space, thereby integrating different module capabilities. Our approach requires no additional training and enables highly flexible module composition.
